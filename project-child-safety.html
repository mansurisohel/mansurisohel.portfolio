Project Overview

The Smart Child Safety System leverages IoT hardware (Arduino microcontroller, GPS module, temperature/panic sensors) to provide continuous monitoring. Designed to give parents real-time alerts when their child strays beyond a safe zone or presses the emergency SOS button. The prototype is fully simulated in Wokwi.

Key Features

  • One-press SOS panic button with <1s alert latency
  • Real-time GPS-based location tracking
  • Geofence zone alerts when child leaves safe area
  • Temperature & environment monitoring sensors
  • Wokwi simulation for rapid hardware prototyping
  • Low-power design for all-day battery life

Technical Approach

The Arduino sketch handles sensor polling, GPS NMEA parsing, and serial communication. A state machine manages normal monitoring vs. alert modes, minimising false positives. The Wokwi simulation replicates hardware behaviour without physical components, enabling fast iteration.

Impact & Future Scope

The system demonstrates how affordable IoT hardware can address real child safety challenges. Future work includes adding cellular connectivity (SIM800L), a companion mobile app with map visualisation, and two-way audio communication between parent and child device.
